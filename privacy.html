Circadian Shifter Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026

Circadian Shifter is an alarm app that helps you move your sleep schedule. It is made by Deimus, an independent developer based in Romania ("I" in this policy).

The short version

  • Circadian Shifter does not collect any personal data. Nothing you enter or do in the app is sent to me or anyone else.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Everything the app saves stays on your phone.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play.

What the app stores on your phone

To work, the app saves the following on your phone only:

  • your alarm plan, alarm times and settings
  • trip plans you create, such as destination and travel dates
  • copies of sound files you choose to use as alarm sounds
  • whether you have unlocked Circadian Shifter Plus

None of this is sent to me or to any server. If your phone's Android backup is turned on, Android may include this data in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is managed by Google under your Google account settings, not by me.

Usage access (optional)

If you turn on automatic wake-time detection, the app asks for Android's Usage access permission. With it, the app reads only the times your screen turned on and off and when your phone was locked and unlocked over the past week or so. It uses those times to estimate when you usually wake up.

  • It does not read which apps you use or anything you do in them.
  • The times are processed on your phone and never leave it.
  • This is off unless you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time in your phone's settings under Usage access.

Permissions

The app asks for these Android permissions:

  • Notifications, alarms and reminders, and full-screen alerts: to ring your alarms on time, including when the phone is locked.
  • Run at startup: to set your alarms again after the phone restarts.
  • Usage access (optional): to estimate your wake time, as described above.

The app does not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Purchases

Circadian Shifter Plus is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google processes the payment; I never see your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether your Google account owns Plus, so it can unlock it on your phone. Google's handling of your purchase is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Deleting your data

Because all data is stored on your phone, you can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app or by clearing its storage in Android settings. I hold no data about you, so there is nothing for me to delete.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
